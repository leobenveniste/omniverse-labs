Privacy Policy

App: PulsePocket (com.omniverselabs.pulse_pocket)
Developer: Omniverse Labs
Last Updated: November 11, 2026

100% Offline-First, Zero Audio & Zero Data Collection:
PulsePocket is built with strict privacy-first principles. Your saved setlist slots, BPM tempos, meter preferences, and haptic settings live exclusively in your device's local storage. We do not access microphones, operate servers, require accounts, or collect, transmit, or sell any personal or performance data.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

PulsePocket does not collect, store remotely, or share any personal or sensitive information, including:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or user accounts.
  • Audio recordings or microphone input (the app contains zero audio/recording engine).
  • Analytics telemetry, crash reporting trackers, advertising IDs, or location data.

2. Local-Only Device Storage & Permissions

All app settings and saved setlist presets are stored strictly offline using your device's local storage (SharedPreferences). Uninstalling PulsePocket permanently deletes all local app data. PulsePocket requests only hardware vibration permission (VIBRATE) to drive silent haptic beat pulses and wake lock control while practicing or performing.

3. Optional Contact Support

If you choose to email us via Settings, your email is handled solely by your chosen mail app and is never collected inside PulsePocket.

4. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Contact Omniverse Labs at leo.benveniste@gmail.com.

100% Fuera de Línea, Sin Audio y Cero Recolección de Datos:
PulsePocket funciona exclusivamente en tu dispositivo. Tus temas guardados de setlist, tempos BPM, compases y ajustes hápticos nunca salen de tu teléfono. No operamos servidores, no pedimos cuentas, no usamos micrófono ni recopilamos datos personales.

Todos los presets de setlist y preferencias permanecen en almacenamiento local (SharedPreferences). PulsePocket solo utiliza vibración física del dispositivo (VIBRATE) para pulsos táctiles silenciosos y mantener la pantalla activa durante el uso.
