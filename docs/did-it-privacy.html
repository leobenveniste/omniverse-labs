Privacy Policy — DidIt

Effective Date: November 11, 2026 · Package: com.omniverselabs.did_it

100% Offline & Zero Internet Permission: DidIt never asks for network access, contains zero ads or analytics SDKs, and keeps all tasks, completion timestamps, and automated flows strictly on your device.

1. Data We Collect

None. Omniverse Labs does not collect, transmit, rent, or sell any personal data, task titles, medication schedules, or usage telemetry.

2. On-Device Local Storage

Your recurring items, chained triggers, multi-step flows, confirmation logbook history, and theme/language preferences are stored locally via Android's private SharedPreferences storage. Uninstalling the app removes all local data from your device.

3. Notifications & Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Used exclusively on-device to fire your recurring reminders and quick Did It / Snooze notification actions.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED / VIBRATE: Used strictly to re-schedule local alarms after device reboot and provide tactile confirmation feedback.
